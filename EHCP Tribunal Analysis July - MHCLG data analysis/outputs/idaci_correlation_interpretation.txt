Pearson r = 0.02 (p = 0.805, n = 151) between IDACI average rank and EHCPs as a % of the SEN population across local authorities in 2025/26. Lower rank indicates GREATER child income deprivation, so in deprivation terms: child income deprivation shows essentially no association with what share of an LA's identified SEN cohort holds an EHCP. Correlation is cross-sectional and says nothing about causation; plausible confounders include LA assessment practice, school composition and parental capacity to pursue statutory assessment. The IDACI vintage predates the 2025/26 SEN census, so deprivation lags the outcome it is compared against.
